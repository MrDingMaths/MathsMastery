algFractAlgDenom — Easy: Monomial denominators (kx) only; multiply both sides by kx, one-step linear remains (2–3 steps). Medium: At least one binomial denominator (ax+b), or numerator with x over monomial; cross-multiply or clear LCD (3–4 steps). Hard: x² denominators where x²-terms cancel to leave linear, or product-of-linear denominators (4–6 steps).
algFractBinomial — Easy: Two binomial fracs, coeff 1 on x in each numerator, denominators ≤8, integer RHS; expand two brackets, collect x, divide (3–4 steps). Medium: ≥1 numerator with coeff >1 on x, or variable/fraction RHS, denominators ≤10; heavier coefficient arithmetic (4–6 steps). Hard: Numerator coefficients ≥3x and/or three binomial fracs (RHS also a fraction), large LCM + sign traps (6–8 steps).
algFractCrossMultiply — Easy: (ax+b)/p=(cx+d)/q, a,c∈{1–5}, small denominators, integer answer after straightforward cross-multiplication (2–3 steps). Medium: Fractional answer, bracket notation k(ax+b) in numerator, or constant added to one side (4–5 steps). Hard: LCD clearing needed before cross-multiplying (fraction±constant=fraction or three-term), negative bracket coefficients (5–7 steps).
algFractMonomial — Easy: All fraction terms same side ax/p±bx/q=c; multiply by LCM, combine x-terms, divide (3 steps). Medium: Type A — one frac each side with constant (collect x across equals), or Type B — three fracs same side (LCM of three denominators); both add a step over Easy (4–5 steps). Hard: Fracs on both sides ax/p±bx/q=cx/r±d; must clear fractions then collect x from both sides (5–6 steps).
elimination — Easy: One variable already matched or opposite-signed; direct add/subtract then substitute, integer answers (2–3 steps). Medium: Multiply exactly one equation (×2–×5) to match coefficients; fraction answers possible (3–5 steps). Hard: Multiply both equations with different multipliers (LCM method); larger coefficients, complex fractional answers (5–7 steps).
equatingCoefficients — Easy: Expand bracket(s), each unknown appears in exactly one coefficient equation — solved independently, no substitution (2–3 steps). Medium: Expand, combine like terms, then sequential solving — find first unknown from one coefficient equation, substitute to find the second (3–4 steps). Hard: Equating coefficients produces a²=k for one unknown, giving two ± values; both unknowns have two corresponding values (4–5 steps).
exponentialNoLogs — Easy: Bases already match or one is a trivially obvious power of the other; exponent is bare x, −x, or kx; always integer answer (1–2 steps). Medium: Must convert at least one base to common base (e.g. 8=2³); fraction/decimal bases; linear exponent after conversion gives fraction answer (2–4 steps). Hard: Multiple base conversions plus multi-step linear equation for exponent; products/quotients of exponentials; simultaneous two-variable exponential systems (3–6 steps).
linearInequalities — Easy: Single operation to isolate x; no sign flip; no negative x coefficient (1 step). Medium: Multiple operations; may require flipping sign; variables on both sides or single bracket expansion (2–4 steps). Hard: Brackets on both sides, or fraction with variable expression on the other side, or compound bracket + fraction expansions (4–6 steps).
mixedMultistepLinear — Easy: Collect like terms on one/both sides, or single bracket expansion (bracket must require expansion — a(bx±c)+dx=e or a(x±b)=c+dx forms). Medium: Expand one or two brackets with like-term collection, variables on both sides, or fractions with binomial numerators. Hard: Fractions on both sides, nested operations, or squared terms that expand and cancel to leave a linear equation.
mixedTwoStep — Easy: Composite pool from twoStep/twoStepNegCoeff/twoStepGrouping Easy tiers (2 steps). Medium: Composite pool from Medium tiers of those families (2–3 steps). Hard: Composite pool from Hard tiers — decimal coefficients, non-trivial fractions, negative fractional coefficients, or multi-term groupings (2–4 steps).
monicQuadratic — Easy: Already in standard form x²+bx+c=0; identify integer factor pairs of c that sum to b; read roots directly (2–3 steps). Medium: Single rearranging step needed before factorising (collect terms, or reverse order like 21x+20+x²=0) (3–4 steps). Hard: Two or more preparatory steps — divide through by common integer factor, expand one/two bracket products, or clear algebraic fraction denominators — then factorise (4–6 steps).
nonMonicQuadratic — Easy: Already in standard form ax²+bx+c=0; direct factorisation to (ax+b)(cx+d)=0; fractional roots expected (2–3 steps). Medium: Non-standard form requiring rearrangement before factorising (3–4 steps). Hard: Decimal or fraction coefficients, rational expressions with x in denominator requiring clearing, or disguised quadratics via substitution (4–6 steps).
nullFactorLaw — Easy: Equation already factored, or trivially factor out x or ax from ax²+bx=0; integer answers (1–2 steps). Medium: Negative leading coefficient, rearrange ax²=bx to ax²−bx=0 first, or non-integer solutions including fraction roots (2–3 steps). Hard: Must clear fraction denominators or expand-and-collect before factoring; surd roots (x²=k pattern) or three-root cubics (3–4 steps).
oneStep — Easy: Single operation only; all coefficients, constants, and answers are positive integers. Medium: Single operation; at least one negative integer appears; answers are integers (possibly negative). Hard: Single operation; answer is non-integer — proper fraction, improper fraction, or terminating decimal.
pronumeralsBothSides — Easy: Collect variable terms to one side via one step; positive integer answers. Medium: Collect variable terms; negative or zero integer answers. Hard: Collect variable terms; fractional or decimal answers.

Hard: Must clear x-denominators (algebraic fractions), square a radical, or substitute (u=2^x, u=x², etc.) before a quadratic emerges (5+ steps).

simpleQuadratic — Easy: x²=k or ax²=k; 1–2 operations before taking the square root. Medium: ax²+b=c; 3 operations (move b, divide by a, root); x² terms all on same side. Hard: x² terms on both sides (ax²+c=bx²+d); 4+ operations — collect x² terms, move constant, divide, root.
substitution — Easy: At least one equation pre-solved for y/x (y=mx+c or x=ay+b); direct substitution gives a linear equation; integer answers (2–3 steps). Medium: One equation needs minor rearrangement before substituting; fraction coefficients allowed; double-root quadratic-linear systems (single solution) included (3–5 steps). Hard: Neither equation pre-solved; non-linear systems (quadratic-linear, circle x²+y²=k, product xy=k) giving two distinct solution pairs (5+ steps).
twoStep — Easy: Positive integer coefficient a∈{2–12} or x/n with n∈{2,3}; integer or half-integer answers (2 steps). Medium: Coefficient up to 25, x/n with n≤10, or fractional ax/b coefficient; any rational answer (2 steps). Hard: Decimal coefficient or decimal constant; or integer equation with non-trivial fraction answer (denominator ≥3) (2 steps).
twoStepGrouping — Easy: a(x±b)=c or (x±b)/a=c; coefficient 1 on x inside bracket; positive multiplier; integer answers (2 steps). Medium: a(bx±c)=d with b>1; negative multipliers; extra terms outside bracket; fractional or integer answers (2–3 steps). Hard: Multi-term combinations — a(bx±c)+dx=e or fraction form with b>1; non-trivial fractional answers; negative multipliers on complex expressions (3 steps).
twoStepNegCoeff — Easy: Negative integer coefficient (−ax or b−ax form), no brackets, integer answers (2 steps). Medium: Coefficient −1, or negative integer coeff with any rational answer, or neg fractional coeff with large integer answer (e.g. −5x/11=15); no brackets (2 steps). Hard: Negative fractional coefficient in two-step (−px/q±b=c), or b−ax=c with non-integer answer; any fraction or decimal answer; no brackets (2–3 steps).
