[multiplyTerms] — Easy: two factors, at most one negative, all degree-1, at most two distinct variables. Medium: two factors both negative (sign-change), OR three factors with ≤2 distinct letter variables, degree-1. Hard: three factors with ≥3 distinct letter variables, OR two/three factors with at least one powered term (degree ≥2).
[addSubtractTerms] — Easy: 2–3 terms, one like-term group, at most one constant bystander, no double-negative constructions. Medium: 3–4 terms with two distinct like-term groups (e.g. x and y), or negative-coefficient results, or commutativity recognition in product variables (ba=ab). Hard: mixed powers of same base (x² and x together), compound power variables (x²y, xy²), double-negative subtraction, 5+ terms across 3+ groups, or hidden cancellation in a complex expression.
[divideTerms] — Easy: 0 index-law reductions, ≤2 distinct variables (coefficient reduction + cancelling at most one shared first-power variable; sign/negatives not a difficulty driver). Medium: exactly 1 index-law reduction (one powered variable reduced, e.g. a²/a) OR 0 reductions but 3+ distinct variables/factors to track. Hard: 2 simultaneous index-law reductions — both numerator and denominator carry powers on two different variables, leaving variables top and bottom plus a coefficient fraction.
[mixedSimplification] — Easy: single-variable, single-operation-type, ≤3 additive terms all same variable, no two-variable products. Medium: two-variable monomials (xy/ab) introduced, OR four-term two-group expressions with no commutativity recognition required, OR two-monomial multiplication with mixed variables. Hard: commutativity recognition (yx=xy) required across like terms, OR three-factor chains, OR 5+ terms across 3+ groups, OR expressions mixing x²y with xy², OR fractions with sign-flip partial cancellation, OR compound multi-variable exponents on both variables simultaneously.
[powerOfPowerAndZeroPower] — Easy: single application of one law (power-of-a-power or zero power) on one variable or numeric base, no coefficient, no combination with other index laws. Medium: coefficient present (stays outside or gets raised with the monomial), or power-of-a-power combined with product/quotient law in two steps, or zero power embedded in an expression requiring arithmetic evaluation. Hard: two or more separately-powered monomials requiring simultaneous coefficient arithmetic and exponent arithmetic, or three-variable products with independent exponent calculations, or multi-step fractions combining power-of-a-power with coefficient reduction on both sides.
[divisionIndexLaw] — Easy: single variable or numerical base, no coefficient, one subtraction step giving a positive integer power result. Medium: coefficient simplifies to a clean integer, and/or two variables each requiring independent index subtraction; inverse cases and simple negatives with clean integer coefficients included. Hard: coefficient division produces a non-integer fraction, OR three distinct variables, OR double negatives, OR negative combined with fractional coefficient or inverse exponent case.
[multiplicationIndexLaw] — Easy: pure index law on a single letter variable, no numerical coefficients, just add exponents, any number of factors. Medium: two factors with explicit numerical coefficients and/or a second distinct letter variable; negative coefficients allowed. Hard: three or more factors with at least one coefficient or multi-variable term; OR fraction coefficients; OR three or more distinct letter variables; OR reordering/commutativity recognition required (e.g. ab×ba).
[expandSingleBrackets] — Easy: positive numeric or positive degree-1 monomial outer factor × two-term bracket, single variable family throughout (no cross-product terms). Medium: negative outer factor (sign-flip required), or two distinct variables producing cross-product terms, or three-term bracket; outer factor degree ≤ 1. Hard: fraction outer coefficient requiring integer simplification, or degree-2+ outer factor (x², -3y²) producing cubic+ results, or variable outer factor × bracket with degree-2 terms.
[mixedIndexLaws] — Easy: apply one index law (×, ÷, power-of-power, zero power) to a single-base expression with no coefficients. Medium: chain two or more laws on a single base with no coefficients (e.g. expand then divide, or multiply then divide). Hard: apply index laws to expressions with numeric coefficients and/or multi-variable monomials, including algebraic fractions or two or more power-of-power applications in the same expression.
[expandBinomialProducts] — Easy: both factors are monic linear binomials (coefficient 1 on the variable), numeric constants only, no scalar outside. Medium: scalar multiplier outside the product, OR at least one non-monic bracket, OR two monic products combined. Hard: two distinct letter variables in the product, OR abstract letter coefficients, OR variable monomial factor producing a cubic, OR two non-monic products combined.
[factoriseIntoSingleBrackets] — Easy: two terms, positive HCF; HCF is either a positive integer or a single degree-1 variable (coefficient 1), no sign management. Medium: two terms; HCF is a monomial with both a coefficient >1 and a variable (e.g. 3x), OR a negative leading term requiring sign-flip. Hard: three or more terms, OR HCF contains two or more distinct letter variables (e.g. ab), OR any variable in the HCF has degree ≥ 2.
[orderOfOperations] — Easy: exactly one × or ÷ evaluated before + or −; degree-1 terms only; at most one subsequent like-term collection. Medium: two or more sequential BODMAS choices required; bracket-vs-no-bracket contrast pairs, fractions, or cross-variable products; degree-1 in problem (degree-2 may appear in answer). Hard: at least one powered term (degree ≥ 2) in the problem; index law applied within the BODMAS chain; multi-variable with exponents or power-of-power.
[expandAndSimplify] — Easy: one bracket with positive outer factor expanded and collected with at most two like-term groups; single variable, no sign-flip from bracket negation. Medium: two degree-1 outer brackets combined with at most one negative outer factor (single variable), or one bracket with a negative outer factor. Hard: two brackets both with negative outer factors, or any expression producing degree-2+ terms in the result, or two-variable cross-bracket like-term collection, or three or more separate expressions combined.
[factoriseMonicQuadraticTrinomials] — Easy: all-positive monic quadratic (b>0, c>0 in x²+bx+c); factor pair search entirely in positive integers; no sign management. Medium: monic quadratic with at least one negative sign; sign-aware factor pair selection required to identify which root is negative. Hard: non-monic leading coefficient with a common integer factor k>1; student must extract k first to reveal k(x²+bx+c), then factorise the inner monic quadratic; inner quadratic may have any sign pattern.
[powerOfProductsAndQuotients] — Easy: single bracket with at most one powered variable inside (or two plain variables); at most one side of a quotient carries an inner exponent. Medium: single bracket with two or more bases each carrying inner exponents, OR a negative base, OR an external factor that merges via index laws after expansion. Hard: two or more separately-raised brackets combined by multiplication, division, or addition/subtraction, requiring cross-bracket index-law simplification to reach the final form.
[addSubtractAlgebraicFractions] — Easy: both fractions share the same numeric denominator; add/subtract numerators directly, simplifying the result if needed. Medium: different numeric denominators (or an integer constant combined with a fraction); find LCD to combine; all variables appear in numerators only. Hard: at least one denominator contains a variable or product of variables, OR two different letter variables appear requiring cross-multiplication, OR denominators mix different powers of the same variable.
[negativeIndices] — Easy: single-step direct application of a^{-n} = 1/aⁿ on one base at a time — isolated single-variable, coefficient × variable, numeric base, or fraction-with-negative-denominator expressions; no index law operations required. Medium: two or more bases with mixed positive/negative indices, or a single application of one index law (×, ÷, or power-of-power) where negative indices appear before or after; includes whole-expression negation like (kx)^{-n}. Hard: two or more sequentially applied index laws in a single expression — multi-step chains combining products, quotients, and powers of bracketed monomials across multiple variables, including fractions-of-fractions and nested powers with negative exponents at each stage.
[expandDifferenceOfTwoSquares] — Easy: coefficient-1 variable and integer constant in each factor; variable or constant may appear first. Medium: at least one term carries a coefficient ≠ 1, higher power, surd, or variable product; both squared terms expand cleanly to integers or rationals. Hard: at least one term is a fraction, reciprocal, or composite binomial expression used as a single term, so squaring requires fraction arithmetic or leaves an unexpanded binomial square.
[expandPerfectSquares] — Easy: single-variable monic linear binomial with integer constant; direct (a+b)² with whole-number arithmetic only. Medium: at least one term has a non-unit integer coefficient, two distinct letter variables appear, or a scalar multiplier sits outside the bracket; all bases are integer-coefficient polynomials. Hard: at least one term is a fraction or reciprocal (variable in denominator), or both terms are degree-≥2 polynomials across two distinct letter variables; requires fraction arithmetic or nested index-law bookkeeping.
[multiplyDivideAlgebraicFractions] — Easy: single × or ÷ with coefficient arithmetic only; no variable cancellation across fractions; degree-1 variables in both inputs and output. Medium: at least one variable cancellation across the two fractions required, OR the output has a squared variable arising from two degree-1 inputs multiplying the same variable; no degree ≥ 2 variable in any input. Hard: degree ≥ 2 variable present in at least one input fraction, OR three or more fractions combined in a chain, OR parenthetical grouping that changes the order of × and ÷.
[addSubtractFractionsWithBinomialNumerator] — Easy: two fractions, numeric denominators, addition only (or subtracting a monomial-only numerator); at least one numerator is a binomial; no sign-distribution challenge. Medium: subtraction requires distributing a negative across a full binomial second numerator, OR three fractions with at least one binomial numerator, OR two-variable binomials. Hard: at least one denominator contains a variable factor; LCD is algebraic, producing polynomial terms in the combined numerator.
[mixedExpansion] — Easy: one expansion technique applied once to integer-coefficient factors; result degree ≤ 3; single variable (or the most basic two-variable forms like (x+y)²); no post-expansion collection beyond the direct expansion. Medium: outer integer scalar multiplied across a binomial product k(ax+b)(cx+d), or two separately-expanded brackets combined and collected, or a triple product monomial × binomial × binomial, or a two-variable binomial × binomial with integer coefficients; no fractional coefficients within factors. Hard: factors involve fractional coefficients (including reciprocal terms 1/x), general literal coefficients (ax+b)(cx+d), or special cubic identities; OR multi-step compositions producing degree ≥ 4 results; OR combinations of three or more bracket groups with two-variable outputs requiring multi-stage collection.
